Frequently Asked Questions

What car types are available? +

Car Lite (sedan/hatchback) and Car Standard (larger vehicles). Both are air-conditioned with verified captains.

How is the fare calculated? +

Fares are based on distance and time. You'll see the estimate before confirming — no hidden charges.

Can I schedule a car in advance? +

Currently on-demand only. Pre-scheduling is coming soon!

Is the car service available 24/7? +

Yes! Availability depends on captains online in your area.

What vehicle is eligible for Pathao Car? +

Any well-maintained car manufactured in 2010 or later with valid registration and insurance.

What is the commission structure? +

Pathao takes a small service commission. The rest goes to you, plus bonus programs.

Can I drive both Bike and Car on Pathao? +

Yes! Register multiple vehicle types under the same account.
