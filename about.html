Our Mission

Making everyday journeys better for every Nepali

From a two-wheeler startup to Nepal's leading super-app for rides, food, and deliveries — Pathao has grown alongside Kathmandu. We believe technology should make life simpler, not harder.

Today we connect hundreds of thousands of customers with riders, restaurants, and businesses every single day — and we're just getting started.

What drives us

Our Values

People First

Every decision — from product to policy — starts with asking how it helps our riders, captains, and customers.

Safety Always

We invest heavily in rider training, 2-way ratings, and insurance coverage so every trip feels secure.

Move Fast

Speed matters — whether it's delivering food or shipping a new feature. We bias toward action.

Fair Earnings

Riders and captains are partners, not contractors. We design our incentive system to reward consistent effort.

Built for Nepal

We're not a copy-paste of a global product. Every feature is designed with Nepali roads, culture, and needs in mind.

Grow Together

When our partners and riders thrive, Pathao thrives. We succeed only when the people on our platform succeed.

Our Journey

From One Bike to a Super-App

15

2015 — The Beginning

Pathao launches in Kathmandu as a bike-hailing app. First rides, first riders, first community.

18

2018 — Food & Car

Pathao Food and Pathao Car launch, transforming Pathao into a multi-service platform.

20

2020 — 1M Rides

Pathao crosses 1 million completed rides, a milestone for Nepal's ride-sharing industry.

22

2022 — Tuktuk Launches

Pathao adds Tuktuk (auto-rickshaw) to its fleet, making short-distance rides even more affordable.

26

2026 — Parcel Service

Pathao Parcel launches — same-day delivery for businesses and individuals across the Valley.
