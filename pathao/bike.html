Frequently Asked Questions

How to request a ride? +

Open the app, tap Bike, set your pickup and drop-off, and confirm!

How can I get a promo code? +

Promo codes are shared on our official social media pages and via push notifications.

I was overcharged. How can I get a refund? +

Go to your ride history, select the trip, and tap "Report an issue." Our team will review within 2-3 business days.

How to use Refer and Earn? +

Tap "Refer & Earn" in your profile. Share your code — when friends complete their first ride, both get rewards!

What if my rider behaved rudely? +

Rate your ride and contact support at 01-5970099 or hello@pathao.com.

How do I register as a rider? +

Visit our Earn with Bike page and fill out the registration form.

When do I get paid? +

Payments are processed weekly. Track earnings in real-time via the Pathao Drive app.

What documents do I need? +

Valid driving license, Citizenship card or passport, and vehicle registration documents.
